Experience

A timeline of my co-op placements — the company above the line, what I did below it.

Amphenol Canada Corp. Markham, ON

One of the world’s largest interconnect manufacturers. Its Canadian division builds high-reliability connectors and motor jacks for aerospace, defence, and commercial systems.

Sept – Dec 2026

Engineering Design StudentCurrent

Modelling and drafting connector components in SolidWorks — manufacturing drawings, new product design through design review, and engineering change notices on released parts.

My first time designing parts that ship by the million — a tenth of a millimetre decides whether a connector mates.

TRIUMF Vancouver, BC

Canada’s national particle accelerator laboratory. Its ARIEL facility produces rare isotopes for physics research and medical isotope development.

Jan – Apr 2026

ARIEL Engineering Assistant

Designed a wall-mounted steel frame for a radioactive gas manifold, sized through iterative ANSYS FEA — 25% less deflection — with GD&T drawings and BOMs managed in SolidWorks PDM.

One small part of a huge project. The lesson wasn’t the FEA — it was keeping my design in sync with everyone else’s, and asking the technicians how they’d build it.

BC Hydro — Site C Fort St. John, BC

British Columbia’s provincial electric utility. Site C is its hydroelectric dam on the Peace River, one of the province’s largest capital projects.

May – Aug 2024

Mechanical Engineering Co-op

Redlined P&IDs and layouts for constructability, verified installations in the field, and turned RFIs and field change forms into revisions across mechanical, electrical, and civil teams.

Living on camp beside the build, I started reading drawings the way the trades do. A design isn’t good until it can be built cleanly.
